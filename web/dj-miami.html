DJ Services FAQ

What DJ services does Miami DJ Beat offer?

We provide DJs for weddings, quinceañeras, clubs and nightlife, corporate events, private and family parties, and themed events such as Halloween, St. Patrick’s Day and the 4th of July, plus bilingual MC hosting.

Do you offer bilingual DJ and MC service?

Yes. Bilingual (English and Spanish) DJ and MC service is available; tell us the language you need when you request your quote.

How do I get a quote or check availability?

Open the page for your event type and request a quote, call (305) 607-1780, or email miamidjbeat@gmail.com.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Miami-Dade County, Broward County and South Florida.

Do you have a DJ for quinceañeras?

Yes — a bilingual DJ and MC for the vals, the court presentation, surprise dances and the full reception, across Miami-Dade and Broward.

How much does a DJ cost in Miami?

It depends on the event type, hours and equipment. As a guide: private events from $500, family parties from $350 and themed parties from $600. Open the page for your event type and request a quote.
