Artist health & economics

The private finance and performance panel on Miami DJ Beat — revenue, events, residency, and a professional health index that goes beyond public reviews.

Every roster artist (LITE or PRO) gets Cash Flow on profile or dashboard. No one else sees your numbers: fans see reviews; you see ledger, KPIs, charts, and bank-style statements. Checks and local pay count when you reflect them in leads or the ledger.

Cash Flow lives on the web (not in the MDJPRO Mac app). Real movements enter via dj_ledger in Supabase — Stripe, staff releases, and accepted SoundForTips™ tips. This page is informative; the live panel is on your profile.

Where Cash Flow sits in MDJB

Sign in → Profile / Dashboard → Flow tab → Range + KPIs → Statement + CSV

What Cash Flow is not

  • It is not public — visitors and other DJs do not see your Flow tab or KPIs.
  • It is not in MDJPRO Mac — it is a Miami DJ Beat web SaaS module.
  • It does not replace SoundForTips™ — live booth and its PRO tab are separate products.
  • It does not auto-import off-platform cash — you must reflect it in leads or ledger lines.

Today vs what comes next

  • Today: KPIs, Chart.js graphs, bank statement, and CSV export from dj_ledger + accepted SFT.
  • You: LITE or PRO artist — full private panel access; PRO unlocks SoundForTips™, not Cash Flow.
  • Next: TICKET-004 architecture — five financial layers per order with unified append-only ledger.
