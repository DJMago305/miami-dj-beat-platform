Dance Lessons FAQ

Do we need dance experience to learn the vals?

No. The choreography is built around the quinceañera and her court, at their pace, whether they have never danced or already do.

Who teaches the lessons?

A partner dance school in Miami-Dade that works with Miami DJ Beat. You request your quote with us and we coordinate the lessons; Miami DJ Beat does not teach dance itself.

Can the court and the father-daughter dance be included?

Yes. Tell us who will dance — the quinceañera, damas, chambelanes and family — when you request your quote.

Can my DJ play the vals exactly as we rehearsed it?

Yes. Share the song and the timing with us and your DJ and MC cue the vals and the court presentation at the right moment.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Miami-Dade County, Broward County and South Florida.

How do I get a quote?

Call (305) 607-1780 or email miamidjbeat@gmail.com. Tell us your date, venue and what you need.
