One Hit Wonder

A touring live DJ show produced by Miami DJ Beat LLC and performed by DJMago305 — a set built entirely around one-hit-wonder anthems that everyone recognizes from the first note, across every generation and culture. Not tied to one club: this production travels to different venues and cities.

The Concept

A curated set of true one-hit-wonder anthems — chosen for global recognition, not chart history. Any decade, any culture, one rule: if the room knows it in the first second, it belongs in the set.

Show Format

Built on the same production standard as our other live shows — professional sound, DJ booth, and lighting sized to the room. Exact technical requirements are confirmed per venue during booking.

Experience & House Rules

Dress code, age policy, and access rules follow the hosting venue for each date — they'll be published alongside the confirmed date and location once announced.
