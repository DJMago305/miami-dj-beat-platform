DJ Animador & MC FAQ

Do you host in both English and Spanish?

Yes. Our MCs move smoothly between English and Spanish, reading the room to switch languages so every guest feels included.

Can you work alongside our own DJ, or do we need to book both roles from you?

Both work — we can bring the DJ and the MC/animator together as one booking, or provide MC and hosting services alongside a DJ you've already booked.

What is hora loca, and is it included?

Hora loca is a high-energy dedicated segment — props, masks, confetti, and a run of peak-tempo music — built into your reception or party program. It's available as part of our animation packages; tell us your vision and we'll build the segment around it.

Do you do corporate hosting as well as private parties?

Yes. We host corporate galas, brand activations, and award ceremonies with the same bilingual crowd-work skill set we bring to weddings and private parties — the tone adjusts, the energy management doesn't. (305) 607-1780 · miamidjbeat@gmail.com
