Wedding Event Blueprint

Segment Details & Operational Direction
1 — Reception & Cocktail Hour

From 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM the atmosphere remains elegant, modern, and upscale. Music direction includes Deep House, Afro House, Organic House, and melodic Tech House selections designed to create a premium Miami-style wedding environment from the first moment guests arrive.

The DJ must establish tone immediately — no abrupt transitions, no heavy drops. The cocktail hour is about ambiance, sophistication, and setting the emotional foundation for the celebration ahead. Volume levels remain conversational throughout this segment.

2 — Wedding Court & Newlywed Entrance

The wedding court presentation begins at 6:00 PM using the custom "Wedding Mix." The DJ must preload the mix and prepare a HOT CUE at the energetic transition point where Iris Casal & Angel Campis make their grand entrance to the center of the dance floor.

The MC coordinates all introductions in sequence, maintaining elegant crowd engagement and synchronized timing with the DJ. Each wedding party member is announced individually before the newlyweds are presented. The HOT CUE activation must be precise — this is the emotional peak of the formal program.

First dance follows immediately after the entrance. Song selection and choreography cues must be confirmed with the couple prior to the event.

Segment Details & Operational Direction
3 — Dinner Service

From 6:30 PM to 8:00 PM guests are guided to their tables for dinner service. Music remains romantic, clean, elegant, and family-friendly — a blend of Cuban classics, crossover pop, nostalgic favorites, and soft modern selections that complement conversation without competing with it.

4 — Open Dance Floor & Hora Loca

At 8:00 PM the MC officially opens the dance floor. If Hora Loca is included, it will be integrated dynamically during this segment. The DJ actively reads the crowd and adapts musical direction throughout the evening — adaptive open-format mixing is essential for 2026 wedding audiences.

The DJ must test multiple musical directions early in this segment and commit to what generates the strongest floor response. Energy management across the full four-hour window is critical — peaks and valleys must be intentional.

5 — Closing Protocol & Load Out

At 12:00 AM the MC formally thanks all guests for celebrating with Iris Casal & Angel Campis. Music energy gradually lowers to support a smooth and elegant guest departure. Following guest exit, the production team begins organized equipment breakdown and load out procedures.
