# MIAMI DJ BEAT — SMS / TEXT MESSAGE TERMS

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

## 1. THE PROGRAM

By providing your phone number and enabling "Text messages (SMS)" in your Account Settings, you consent to receive text messages from **Miami DJ Beat LLC** at the phone number you provided.

## 2. WHAT YOU WILL RECEIVE

Miami DJ Beat sends **transactional, account-related messages only** — not marketing blasts. Examples include:
- Booking confirmations and updates for your event.
- Appointment or event reminders.
- Account and security notices.

Message frequency varies depending on your bookings and account activity.

## 3. RATES & FREQUENCY

**Message and data rates may apply.** Message frequency varies. Consult your mobile carrier's plan for details.

## 4. HOW CONSENT IS COLLECTED

Consent is collected at two points in the Platform:
- **Account creation**: when you register and provide your phone number.
- **Account Settings**: the "Text messages (SMS)" toggle under Notification Preferences, tied to the phone number on your profile.

You may withdraw consent at any time by turning off the SMS toggle in Account Settings, or by replying **STOP** to any message from us. To get help, reply **HELP** or contact us at **miamidjbeat@gmail.com**.

## 5. NO DATA SHARING FOR MARKETING

Your phone number is never sold or shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes. See our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for full details on how we handle your information.

## 6. CARRIERS

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
