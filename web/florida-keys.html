Florida Keys DJ FAQ

Do you charge a travel fee for events in the Florida Keys?

Travel fees may apply depending on the exact location in the Keys and the scope of equipment involved — this is included as a line item in your custom quote, never a surprise on the invoice.

What power requirements do you need for a beach or outdoor event?

We coordinate power access (or generator needs, if the location requires one) directly with you and your venue ahead of time, based on the sound and lighting equipment your event calls for.

Can we do planning meetings remotely if we're not local to Miami?

Yes. We coordinate planning calls and video meetings for clients planning a destination event in the Keys, so you don't need to be in Miami to plan your day.
