Entertainment FAQ

What event entertainment does Miami DJ Beat offer besides DJs?

Hora Loca shows, clowns and kids’ entertainment, and photo, video, drone and 360 booth coverage. We also offer live music and event staff.

Can I combine entertainment with a DJ or other services?

Yes. Tell us what you need for your event when you request a quote and we will put the right combination together.

How do I get a quote or check availability?

Open the page for the entertainment you want and request a quote, call (305) 607-1780, or email miamidjbeat@gmail.com.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Miami-Dade County, Broward County and South Florida.

What is Hora Loca and how much does it cost?

Hora Loca is a high-energy show with costumed performers and live percussion: LED robot, Brasil samba, Cubana, Character and Hadas packages, from $550 to $850.

What can I hire to capture my event?

Photography, videography, drone coverage, a 360 photo booth and a Magic Mirror, from $250 to $400.

Do you have entertainment for kids parties?

Yes, clown shows with different characters, a kids magic show, balloon twisting, face painting, circus-style acts and seasonal Santa appearances, quoted by setup.
