Quinceañera DJ FAQ

How do you coordinate the choreographed waltz (vals)?

We cue your vals track precisely to your choreography — send us the song and the rehearsed timing, and we'll coordinate lighting and transitions with you or your choreographer so the entrance and dance land exactly on beat.

Do you help coordinate the court (damas y chambelanes)?

Yes. Our bilingual MC handles the court's grand entrance, introductions, and any surprise choreography, working directly with you and your court beforehand so every cue is clear on the day.

Can you plan a surprise dance or dynamic?

Absolutely — surprise dances, a shoe-change moment, hora loca props, or a choreographed reveal with the court. Tell us what you're planning and we'll build the music and timing around it.

Do you offer bilingual MC services?
