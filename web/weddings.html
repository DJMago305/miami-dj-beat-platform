Wedding DJ FAQ

How do we choose the music for our wedding?

We build your playlist together — you share your must-plays and do-not-plays, and your MC/DJ curates the rest live based on the room, keeping your ceremony, cocktail hour, and reception flowing naturally.

How do you coordinate timing with our ceremony and reception?

We coordinate directly with you (and your planner or coordinator, if you have one) on a timeline for ceremony, cocktail hour, and reception cues — introductions, first dance, toasts, and cake cutting.

Do you provide backup equipment or technical support?

Yes. Our sound and microphone setups are checked before your event, and our team is on-site to handle technical support throughout your ceremony and reception.
