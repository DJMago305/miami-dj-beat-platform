**MIAMI DJ BEAT LLC — PLATFORM RULES**

### 1. Professional Standards

Users must provide accurate profiles, respond promptly, honor confirmed commitments, and maintain professional communication. Misrepresentation may result in termination.

### 2. On-Site Conduct

Performers must arrive on time, conduct services professionally, and comply with venue rules. **No third-party agency branding** on attire, equipment, or handouts during Platform events unless pre-approved. Default dress: Miami DJ Beat Standard (all-black or corporate-neutral) unless Event Blueprint specifies otherwise.

### 3. Safety

Performers may **pause or stop performance** if subjected to harassment, unsafe conditions, or threats. Clients must provide reasonable security cooperation. Report incidents to Platform within four (4) hours.

*Concept: performer safety stop-work — adapted from reference service contracts; US framing.*

### 4. Equipment and Venue

Performers responsible for own equipment unless otherwise contracted. Platform and Performers not liable for venue power outages, weather outdoor cancellations, or venue equipment failures unless caused by Performer negligence. Clients responsible for damage to Performer equipment caused by guests where documented.

### 5. Communication

Use Platform messaging for booking-related communication when possible. Do not share client PII externally. Staff may monitor communications for compliance.

### 6. Reviews and Ratings

Honest reviews permitted. Fake reviews, retaliation, or review manipulation prohibited.

### 7. Prohibited Content

No illegal content, hate speech, or explicit material in public profiles without age-gated approval.

### 8. Enforcement

Warnings, suspension, payout holds, termination. Severe violations referred to legal action.
