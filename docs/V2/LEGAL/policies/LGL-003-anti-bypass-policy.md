**MIAMI DJ BEAT LLC — ANTI-BYPASS POLICY**

**Standalone Policy — Must remain clearly visible in Platform legal center**

### 1. Purpose

Miami DJ Beat LLC invests substantial resources in marketing, advertising, technology, personnel, negotiations, and client acquisition. This Policy protects that investment by prohibiting parties from using the Platform to obtain contacts or opportunities and then contracting **off-platform** to avoid Platform fees, protections, and oversight.

### 2. Definitions

**"Introduction"** means a connection between a Performer (or Vendor) and a Counterparty first established through the Platform, including via matching, staff-facilitated inquiry, proposal, or confirmed booking.  
**"Counterparty"** means a Client, venue, restaurant, club, hotel, corporate entity, event organizer, or affiliate introduced to the Performer through the Platform.  
**"Protected Period"** means twenty-four (24) months from the Introduction date unless a shorter period is stated in a signed waiver.  
**"Circumvention"** means soliciting, negotiating, quoting, accepting payment, or performing substantially similar entertainment or event services with a Counterparty outside Platform payment and booking systems during the Protected Period.

### 3. What Constitutes a Valid Introduction

A valid Introduction occurs when **any** of the following is recorded in the Introduction Registry:

(a) Platform matching assigns or presents a Counterparty to a Performer;  
(b) A Client inquiry, lead, or order is linked to a Performer profile;  
(c) An Event Service Agreement (CTR-006) or booking deposit connects the parties;  
(d) Staff documents an introduction in CRM with timestamp and source.

Mere public browsing of a Performer profile by an unknown visitor **without** Platform-facilitated contact exchange is **not** an Introduction until (a)–(d) occurs.

### 4. When Protection Begins

Protection begins on the **Introduction date** recorded in the Registry. For bookings, the earlier of match acceptance or deposit payment may serve as Introduction date.

### 5. Protected Relationships

During the Protected Period, the following relationships are protected:

- Performer ↔ private Client  
- Performer ↔ corporate Client  
- Performer ↔ venue, club, restaurant, or hotel contact  
- Performer ↔ event organizer introduced via Client or venue  
- Vendor ↔ any Counterparty where Introduction involved Platform event  

**Affiliates:** Counterparties include entities and individuals acting on behalf of the introduced Counterparty (e.g., event planner for corporate Client).

### 6. Restricted Conduct

Without **prior written authorization** from Miami DJ Beat LLC, you shall not:

(a) Offer or accept off-platform pricing for protected services;  
(b) Exchange personal contact for purpose of bypassing Platform fees;  
(c) Use the Platform primarily to **discover** contacts for off-platform business;  
(d) Refer introduced Counterparties to third-party agencies to avoid fees;  
(e) Split invoices to underreport GBV;  
(f) Perform "private" gigs for introduced Counterparties without booking.

**Clients** shall not solicit Performers introduced through the Platform for off-platform engagements during the Protected Period.  
**Venues and restaurants** shall not contract directly with Platform-introduced Performers for events that originated through MDJB introductions.

### 7. Authorized Exceptions

Off-platform engagement is permitted **only** when:

- Miami DJ Beat LLC issues a written **Bypass Waiver** (signed by authorized management) referencing the Introduction ID; or  
- The Protected Period has expired and no superseding agreement applies; or  
- The relationship predated Platform records **and** is documented by pre-existing evidence accepted by MDJB.

Verbal exceptions are **invalid**.

### 8. Consequences

Violations may result in:

- Immediate suspension or termination of Platform access;  
- Withholding of pending payouts during investigation;  
- Recovery of lost commissions and documented replacement costs;  
- **Liquidated damages** (subject to counsel approval): the greater of (i) two (2) times the GBV of the circumvented engagement, or (ii) three thousand dollars ($3,000 USD), where enforceable under Florida law;  
- Authorization for offset against future earnings where permitted;  
- Referral to legal counsel for injunctive relief and collection.

### 9. Introduction Registry

MDJB maintains an Introduction Registry (see `docs/V2/LEGAL/README.md`) recording performer, counterparty, venue, date, source, status, and expiration. Users may request their Introduction records via support.

### 10. Enforcement and Cooperation

Users must cooperate with investigations. False statements constitute material breach. MDJB may monitor booking patterns, duplicate venue/client pairings, and payment anomalies.

### 11. Relationship to Other Agreements

This Policy is incorporated into Terms of Service, DJ Partner Agreement, Artist Agreement, Client agreements, and Event Service Agreements. In conflict, the most protective provision for MDJB marketplace integrity applies unless counsel directs otherwise.
