**MIAMI DJ BEAT LLC — PRIVACY POLICY**

**Last Updated:** {last_updated}

Miami DJ Beat LLC ("**Platform**," "**we**") respects your privacy. This Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our website, client portal, artist dashboard, staff tools, and related services.

### 1. Information We Collect

**Clients:** name, email, phone, billing address, event details, payment method tokens (via processor), communications.  
**Performers:** legal name, stage name, bio, photos, rider, availability, location/radius, tax forms (W-9), payout details, performance history.  
**Vendors:** business name, contacts, insurance/license uploads.  
**Automatic:** device data, logs, cookies, IP (hashed for audit), usage analytics.  
**Staff-operated systems:** introduction records, contract audit trails.

### 2. How We Use Information

Matching and booking; payments and payouts; contracts and legal compliance; anti-bypass enforcement; customer support; optional marketing (with consent); security and fraud prevention; aggregated analytics.

### 3. How We Share Information

With matched parties **as required for confirmed events** under staged visibility rules. With payment processors, hosting providers, and counsel when required. We **do not sell** client lead lists to third-party agencies. Performers may not export client databases off-platform (see LGL-003).

### 4. Data Minimization and Staged Visibility

**Pre-booking:** Performers see general area, date, genre — not full client identity.  
**Post-booking / PAID_FULL / T-3:** venue address and operational contacts released as required.  
Access codes and sensitive venue data limited to matched Performer and authorized staff.

### 5. Security

Encryption in transit (TLS). Access controls and row-level security in production systems. W-9 and tax identifiers stored with enhanced controls (see SPC-001). No system is 100% secure; report concerns to support.

### 6. Retention

Account data while active plus period required for tax, contracts, and disputes (typically seven (7) years for transactional records). Legal agreements retained per counsel direction.

### 7. Your Rights

Access, correction, deletion requests subject to legal retention exceptions. Opt-out of marketing. Florida residents may have additional rights under applicable state law — contact us to exercise rights.

### 8. Children

Platform not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

### 9. International Users

Platform operated from United States. Data processed in US. By using the Platform from outside the US, you consent to transfer and processing in the US.

### 10. Changes

Material changes notified via email or Platform notice. Continued use after effective date constitutes acceptance of updated Policy.

### 11. Contact

**Miami DJ Beat LLC** · **miamidjbeat@gmail.com**
