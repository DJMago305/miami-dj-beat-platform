**MIAMI DJ BEAT LLC — CANCELLATION POLICY**

### 1. Booking Commitment

Once an event reaches **BOOKED** status (deposit paid), the event date is reserved. Cancellation disrupts marketplace operations and may harm other users.

### 2. Client Cancellation

| Timing | Consequence |
|--------|-------------|
| After BOOKED, more than 14 days before event | Deposit (30%) **non-refundable**; balance cancelled if not yet charged |
| 14 to 3 days before event | Deposit forfeited; up to 50% of GBV may be charged as cancellation fee per event agreement |
| Within 72 hours (T-3) of event | Full GBV may be forfeited; balance charged if not already collected |
| No-show | Full GBV forfeited; no refund |

Refunds, if any, at Platform discretion minus documented costs and payment processor fees.

### 3. Performer Cancellation

Performer cancellation after BOOKED is **material breach** unless Force Majeure (§7). Performer liable for: emergency replacement costs; lost Platform commission if Client refund required; potential suspension. Repeated cancellations trigger roster review.

### 4. Postponement

Postponement requests must be submitted in writing through Platform at least **fourteen (14) days** before event. Approval not guaranteed. Deposits may transfer once per event if approved. Last-minute postponement treated as cancellation (§2).

*Concept adapted from operational need (reference: advance-notice postponement clauses) — US terms.*

### 5. Replacement Protocol

If Performer fails or is unavailable, Platform will attempt replacement from roster. Client may accept replacement or receive refund per §2 minus non-recoverable costs. Off-platform replacements void Platform protections.

### 6. Vendor / Venue Cancellations

Venue closure or vendor failure: handled under Event Service Agreement and Force Majeure. Platform not liable for venue acts outside its control.

### 7. Force Majeure

Neither party liable for failure due to events beyond reasonable control (hurricane, government order, utility failure at venue not attributable to Performer). Notice within four (4) hours. Documentation may be required. Does not include Performer convenience or better offer.

### 8. Refund Processing

Approved refunds processed to original payment method within ten (10) to fifteen (15) business days.
