**MIAMI DJ BEAT LLC — PAYMENT POLICY**

### 1. Definitions

**GBV** — Gross Booking Value. **Net Payout** — GBV minus Platform commission and approved offsets. **BOOKED** — event state after deposit clears.

### 2. Client Payment Structure

| Milestone | Amount | Timing |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Deposit | 30% of GBV | Due at booking confirmation |
| Balance | 70% of GBV | Auto-charged 72 hours (T-3) before event |
| Minimum deposit | $150 USD | When 30% calculation is lower |

Valid payment method required on file. Prices quoted through Platform are inclusive of stated service scope unless itemized.

### 3. Currency and Processors

USD unless otherwise stated. Processed via approved third-party payment processors. Platform does not store full card numbers.

### 4. Performer Payouts

Net Payout released after: (a) event marked complete; (b) no open dispute; (c) W-9 on file where required; (d) compliance with agreements. Payout timing typically within five (5) to ten (10) business days post-completion unless hold for investigation.

### 5. Platform Commission

Commission rates depend on performer tier, PRO status, and annual GBV tiers as published in program policies. Commissions deducted before Net Payout.

### 6. Adjustments and Offsets

Documented chargebacks, cancellation fees, replacement costs, and enforceable liquidated damages may offset pending payouts with notice when practicable.

### 7. Chargebacks and Disputes

Clients must use internal dispute process before chargeback. Unauthorized chargebacks may incur administrative and recovery fees. Performers must cooperate with payment investigations.

### 8. Taxes

Performers are independent contractors responsible for their taxes. Clients may receive invoices as provided by Platform. Form 1099 reporting requires valid W-9 (SPC-001).

### 9. Records and Audit

Transaction history available in portals. Platform maintains ledger records for audit and tax purposes.

### 10. Changes

Policy updates published with notice. Bookings confirmed under prior policy may honor prior terms for that event only.
