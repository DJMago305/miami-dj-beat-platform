**VENUE COORDINATION AGREEMENT**

**Miami DJ Beat LLC** ("Platform") and **{venue_legal_name}** ("Venue")

Address: {venue_address} · Contact: {venue_contact}

### 1. Purpose

Venue hosts events coordinated by Platform. This Agreement sets operational and legal terms for access, production, and commercial integrity.

### 2. Event Schedule

Load-in: {load_in_time}. Performance hours per Event Service Agreement (CTR-006). Strike per venue rules.

### 3. Venue Responsibilities

(a) Provide safe, lawful access; (b) maintain adequate **power** for agreed technical rider; (c) comply with capacity and fire codes; (d) maintain valid liquor and business licenses where applicable ({liquor_license_ref}); (e) designate venue contact on event date.

### 4. Platform / Performer Responsibilities

Performers comply with venue house rules. Platform coordinates logistics through Event Coordinator.

### 5. Fees

Venue fees (if any) per Event Exhibit. No side payments to Performers outside Platform for Platform-origin events.

### 6. Anti-Bypass

Venue shall not contract directly with Performers or Artists **introduced through Platform** for substantially similar services during Protected Period (LGL-003) without written MDJB waiver.

### 7. Liability

Venue liable for venue infrastructure failures (power, structural hazards) except where Performer caused damage. Each party indemnifies for its negligence.

### 8. Insurance

Venue maintains property and general liability insurance. COI on request (SPC-002).

### 9. Damage

Venue not responsible for Performer equipment loss due to guest theft unless Venue failed reasonable security.

### 10. Cancellation

Venue closure Force Majeure may trigger postponement per LGL-005.

### 11. Data

Venue contact info used only for event operations per LGL-002.

### 12. Governing Law

Florida.
