**DJ PARTNER AGREEMENT**

This DJ Partner Agreement ("**Agreement**") is entered into as of **{effective_date}** by and between **Miami DJ Beat LLC**, a Florida limited liability company ("**Platform**"), and **{contractor_legal_name}** ("**Contractor**" or "**DJ**").

**Stage / Brand Name (display only):** {stage_name}

### RECITALS

WHEREAS, Platform operates a marketplace investing in marketing, technology, and client acquisition; and  
WHEREAS, Contractor desires to offer DJ services through the Platform as an independent business;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree:

### 1. Representations

**Contractor represents:** (a) legal authority to enter this Agreement; (b) professional capacity to perform DJ services; (c) compliance with applicable laws and licensing; (d) accurate profile information; (e) if entity, duly organized and in good standing.

**Platform represents:** it provides marketplace infrastructure subject to these terms.

### 2. Independent Contractor

Contractor is an **independent contractor**, not an employee. Contractor controls method and means of performance, provides equipment unless otherwise agreed, and is solely responsible for taxes, benefits, and insurance.

### 3. Services

Contractor may accept event bookings through Platform. Services include performance, professional setup within agreed scope, and cooperation with MDJB Event Coordinator when assigned.

### 4. Payment

Governed by Payment Policy (LGL-004). Client deposit 30%; balance T-3. Net Payout after event completion minus commission. Contractor authorizes Platform to process payments on Contractor's behalf as marketplace facilitator.

### 5. Cancellations

Governed by Cancellation Policy (LGL-005). Booking after BOOKED is firm commitment. Contractor cancellation is material breach except Force Majeure.

### 6. Failure and Replacement

Contractor liable for documented replacement costs and indemnification for Client refunds caused by Contractor failure.

### 7. Anti-Bypass

Contractor incorporates and agrees to Anti-Bypass Policy (LGL-003). **Restricted Period:** twenty-four (24) months from Introduction. No direct negotiation of pricing or services with introduced Counterparties off-platform.

**Liquidated Damages (subject to counsel):** For circumvention, Contractor owes the greater of 2× circumvented GBV or $3,000 USD where enforceable.

**Offset Authorization:** Contractor authorizes deduction from pending payouts for documented penalties and indemnities where permitted by law.

### 8. Branding and Conduct

No third-party DJ company branding on-site. Miami DJ Beat Standard attire unless Event Blueprint specifies. Platform Rules (LGL-006) incorporated.

### 9. Confidentiality

Contractor shall not disclose Client PII, venue access codes, or Platform operational data. Survives termination.

### 10. Intellectual Property

Contractor retains performance IP. Client event recordings subject to SPC-005 if applicable. Platform trademarks require written permission.

### 11. Insurance

Contractor should maintain general liability insurance. Proof may be required (SPC-002). Platform is not insurer.

### 12. Term and Termination

Effective until terminated. Platform may suspend for breach, circumvention, or quality failures. Contractor may terminate with thirty (30) days notice; obligations survive for booked events and Anti-Bypass.

### 13. Non-Disparagement

No false disparagement of Platform in public forums.

### 14. Force Majeure

As LGL-005. Four (4) hour notice.

### 15. Limitation of Liability

Platform liability capped at commissions earned from Contractor in six (6) months preceding claim.

### 16. Governing Law and Disputes

Florida law. Binding arbitration Miami-Dade County. Attorneys' fees to prevailing party where allowed.

### 17. Entire Agreement

Incorporates LGL-001–006. Supersedes prior DJ service terms for Platform bookings.
